# TRaSH guide custom formats

The JSON files in this folder are unmodified copies of Radarr custom format definitions published by the
[TRaSH Guides](https://github.com/TRaSH-Guides/Guides) project (`docs/json/radarr/cf/`), fetched on 2026-09-10.
They are distributed under the MIT License, copyright the TRaSH Guides contributors.

They serve as the acceptance corpus of the custom format engine: importing them unchanged and matching the same
releases as Radarr is the criterion of milestone M3.
